We ablate 100 randomly sampled programs from Table~\ref{tab:dataset_overview}: 70 loop-only and 30 requiring full specifications. Configurations are token-matched, with at most three refinement iterations; Section~\ref{sec:large-scale-experiment} separately tests the same components on 400 goal-independent programs without that compute cap. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} compares the LLM alone (Pass@3), symbolic guidance without refinement (Pass@3), and the full pipeline (Pass@1).

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} shows high syntax rates but low validity and accuracy for the LLM alone. Symbolic guidance raises validity/accuracy by about 28 percentage points on average; refinement then raises loop-only validity to 95.71\% and function-level validity from 50.00\% to 86.67\%. The latter is the largest gain and, as Section~\ref{sec:large-scale-experiment} shows, the main cost driver.

\begin{finding}
\textbf{Finding (RQ3).} Symbolic-execution guidance and verifier-guided refinement contribute distinct, complementary gains: over the LLM alone, symbolic execution adds ${\sim}28$\,pp of validity/accuracy, and refinement then lifts validity to $95.7\%$ on loop invariants and to $86.7\%$ on full specifications.
\end{finding}
