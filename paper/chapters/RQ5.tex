\subsection{RQ5: How often does \toolname\ produce a \emph{Valid} specification?}
\label{subsec:rq5-valid}

\input{chapters/large_experiment_table}

Table~\ref{tab:large_experiment_summary} shows that full \toolname\ has the
highest C/ACSL \emph{Valid} rate on every model: \(68.0\%\) on gpt-4o,
\(83.3\%\) on gpt-5-mini, \(93.3\%\) on gpt-5.4-mini, and \(93.0\%\) on
gpt-5. In that order, its gains are \(+55.0\), \(+64.3\), \(+66.8\), and
\(+79.2\)\,pp over Pure LLM, and \(+39.2\), \(+57.3\), \(+29.5\), and
\(+21.0\)\,pp over \autospec. Refinement alone adds \(+36.5\), \(+56.5\),
\(+60.3\), and \(+57.0\)\,pp, at the cost of repeated LLM and verifier calls.

Without refinement, symbolic guidance raises \emph{Valid} over Pure LLM on
every model: from \(13.0\%\) to \(31.5\%\) on gpt-4o, \(19.0\%\) to
\(26.8\%\) on gpt-5-mini, \(26.5\%\) to \(33.0\%\) on gpt-5.4-mini,
and \(13.8\%\) to \(36.0\%\) on gpt-5. The unusually low gpt-5 Pure LLM
rate is mainly syntactic: its uses of advanced ACSL behaviors and axiomatics
often have malformed syntax, causing whole-file parse failures. Supplying
local symbolic context before synthesis helps avoid these failures without
repeated LLM sampling. For loop-free programs supported by the flattened
memory model, symbolic execution can directly compute path-sensitive
postconditions without an LLM call.

\rev{On \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini}, refinement gains saturate near \(k=5\);
Houdini adds \(+14.1\)\,pp, and differential prompts with rollback add about
23\,pp over plain error-plus-code repair (\appref{app:refine-ablation},
Fig.~\supref{fig:refine-ablation}).}

\begin{finding}
\textbf{Finding (RQ5).} On \textbf{\toolname-400} the full pipeline reaches $68$--$93\%$ \emph{Valid}; refinement is the dominant but most expensive source of gain, whereas symbolic-execution guidance is a cheaper one that needs no repeated LLM sampling.
\end{finding}
