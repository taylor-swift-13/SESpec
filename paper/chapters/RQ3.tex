To assess the individual contributions of each component, we conducted an ablation study on 100 programs randomly sampled from the benchmark families in Table~\ref{tab:dataset_overview}, following that table's split into 70 loop-only programs (invariant generation only) and 30 programs requiring full specifications (function-level cases). For a fair comparison under controlled compute, all configurations are matched on LLM token usage and the refinement loop is capped at 3 iterations. This token-matched ablation isolates each component on the goal-dependent benchmarks; the complementary ablation in Section~\ref{sec:large-scale-experiment} re-measures the same components on the 400-program goal-independent benchmark without a compute cap.

We compare three configurations: \textbf{Only LLM (Pass@3)} uses the LLM alone for invariant generation without external guidance; \textbf{LLM + symbolic execution (Pass@3)} adds symbolic-execution-derived templates and program context; and \textbf{LLM + symbolic execution + refinement (Pass@1)} adds the verifier-guided refinement loop on top.

The ablation in Table~\ref{tab:ablation} shows that the LLM alone yields high syntactic correctness but low validity and accuracy. Adding symbolic execution lifts validity and accuracy substantially in both settings (average $+28$ percentage points, abbreviated pp) while reducing syntactic correctness only marginally on the loop-only setting ($-1.79$\,pp). Adding the refinement loop pushes loop-only validity to 95.71\% and lifts function-level validity from 50.00\% to 86.67\%, demonstrating that each component contributes a distinct and complementary gain. The largest individual jump comes from the refinement loop, which converts symbolic-execution-derived candidates that the verifier rejects into verifier-checked ones; this is consistent with the cost analysis in Section~\ref{sec:large-scale-experiment}, where refinement is also the dominant cost driver.

\begin{finding}
\textbf{Finding (RQ3).} Symbolic-execution guidance and verifier-guided refinement contribute distinct, complementary gains: over the LLM alone, symbolic execution adds ${\sim}28$\,pp of validity/accuracy, and refinement then lifts validity to $95.7\%$ on loop invariants and to $86.7\%$ on full specifications.
\end{finding}
